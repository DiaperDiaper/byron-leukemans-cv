VeriCore and the case for secure digital identity.

My Information Management 1B business plan explored biometric identity verification as a practical response to fraud, compliance, and trust problems in modern organisations.

Biometric verification POPIA compliance Published 2 May 2026

VeriCore Technologies helped me connect information management to a business problem: organisations need reliable ways to verify identity while protecting personal information. In South Africa, that also means thinking seriously about POPIA compliance and responsible data handling.

The project made me see identity verification as more than a technical service. It is a trust layer. If a system handles biometric data, it must be secure, transparent, and useful enough for clients to believe in it.

This is one of the reasons I am interested in data science and coding. The best systems are not only clever. They solve real risks, explain their value clearly, and make people safer.
