Green economy thinking as a management challenge.

My Public Management and Governance 1B assignment explored South Africa's move toward sustainable development, renewable energy, and policy choices that support long-term growth.

Sustainable development Renewable energy Published 2 May 2026

The green economy topic showed me that sustainability is not separate from management. It affects planning, investment, infrastructure, employment, and the way governments and businesses measure progress.

South Africa's transition requires policy clarity and practical implementation. Renewable energy, resource efficiency, and social development have to work together, otherwise sustainability becomes a slogan instead of a system.

I see this as an information problem too. Decision-makers need reliable data, clear reporting, and systems that show whether policy choices are actually producing economic and environmental results.
